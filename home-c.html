From payment to settlement. Three steps, no chasing.

Your customer pays, it’s checked and confirmed in real time, and the money is settled the next working day and reconciled for you.

  1. 01 · Collect

    Your customer pays, any way they like

    On your website, by bank transfer, by scanning your NQR code, with a payment link on WhatsApp, or with a tap on an Upperlink terminal. One gateway takes them all.

    • Visa, Mastercard, Verve, Amex, UnionPay
    • Transfer to a virtual account
    • NQR
    • POS
    • Payment links and pages
    • NGN and USD
  2. 02 · Check and confirm

    Checked and confirmed in real time

    Payments are confirmed while your customer is still on the page. Each one passes 3-D Secure and the limits set for your business, and our monitoring team reviews anything unusual every day. A transfer is confirmed the moment it lands in its virtual account.

    • Real-time confirmation
    • Transaction limits and restrictions
    • Daily fraud monitoring
    • 3-D Secure
  3. 03 · Settle and reconcile

    Settled next day, reconciled for you

    Money settles to the account you nominate on a T+1 basis, the next working day, or T+2 if you prefer. Every payment is matched to its reference with a daily breakdown of amounts and fees, so reconciliation is done before your finance team opens a spreadsheet.

    • Automatic settlement
    • Split settlements
    • Reconciliation reports
    • Exports for auditors
    • T+1 or T+2 settlement

    Questions, answered

    Who can use Upperlink PayGate?
    Registered companies, business names, schools and institutions, and government agencies and MDAs in Nigeria that need to collect payments online, at the counter, or both.
    Can small businesses use PayGate?
    Yes. A business registered as a business name onboards with a shorter document list. Talk to us if you’re not sure which applies to you.
    Can I add PayGate to my website or app?
    Yes. Use a hosted checkout or payment link with no code, or integrate our API for full control. Webhooks keep your system updated.
    How do I receive the money I collect?
    Settlements are paid on a T+1 basis (the next working day) into the bank account you nominate, or T+2 if you prefer. Each day you can download a breakdown of every amount and fee. With split settlements, each share goes to its own account.
    A payment was successful but hasn’t been settled. What should I do?
    Check its settlement status in your dashboard, then contact support with the transaction reference and we’ll trace it.
    My customer was debited but the payment failed. What happens?
    Share the transaction reference with support. Failed debits are reversed to the customer through their bank. reversal timeline to confirm
    Can I see my transaction history?
    Yes. Filter every payment by date, channel and status, and download records as CSV or PDF.
