Something wrong with a payment?

Choose what happened. We’ll tell you what to check first and what to send us.

A payment shows successful but hasn’t reached your account

  1. In the dashboard, open the payment and check its settlement status.
  2. Check the settlement account on the payment. Split payments settle to several accounts.
  3. Settlement runs T+1 (the next working day), or T+2 if that’s what you chose. If it’s later than that, contact support.
Send us
  • Transaction reference
  • Payment date and amount
  • Your settlement account (last 4 digits)

Your customer was debited but the payment failed

  1. Look up the payment in the dashboard. Failed payments show code 02 or 06.
  2. Failed debits are reversed to the customer by their bank. reversal timeline to confirm
  3. If the customer hasn’t been refunded after that, contact support.
Send us
  • Transaction reference
  • Customer’s bank and date of debit
  • A copy of the debit alert if you have it

You can’t sign in to the dashboard

  1. Use Forgot password to reset it by email.
  2. After five wrong tries, sign-in locks for 15 minutes.
  3. If a teammate left, the account owner can change their access in Team roles.
Send us
  • Your sign-in email
  • Business name

Common questions

How do I receive the money I collect?
Settlements are paid T+1 (the next working day), or T+2 if you prefer, into the bank account you nominate. You can download a daily breakdown of amounts and fees. With split settlements, each share goes to its own account.
Can I change my settlement account?
Yes. The account owner requests the change in the dashboard and we verify it before the next settlement. process to confirm
How do I get POS terminals?
Talk to sales. We deploy, configure and support terminals under our CBN PTSP licence.
Where do I find my API keys?
Sign in to the dashboard and open Settings → API keys. See Before you begin.
How do I report fraud?
Email support with the transaction reference straight away and we’ll start an investigation. fraud reporting channel to confirm
