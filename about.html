Five reasons to collect with PayGate.

Not features. Facts about who we are, and what that means for your money.

See our licences
  1. 01

    Both licences, one company

    We are licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria as a Payment Solution Service Provider for online payments and a Payment Terminal Service Provider for POS. Online and counter payments come from the same company: one contract, one support line, one settlement.

    CBN PSSP · CBN PTSP

  2. 02

    Plugged straight into the national switch

    As a NIBSS aggregator we connect directly to Nigeria’s interbank settlement system, so transfers are matched and confirmed without a chain of middlemen.

    NIBSS aggregator

  3. 03

    Trusted with public money

    Revenue agencies, health insurers and universities collect through PayGate, from thousands of payers at a time, with records that stand up to audit.

  4. 04

    Settlement that follows your rules

    Split every payment by amount or percentage and send each share to its own account automatically. Schools, agencies and marketplaces stop reconciling by hand.

    Split settlements built in

  5. 05

    People you can actually call

    Our support and integration team works from Lagos, in your hours. Large accounts get a named account manager. support hours to confirm
