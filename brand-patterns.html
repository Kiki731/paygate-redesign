Stripe patterns · for approval · not used on the site yet

Three stripe patterns built from Upperlink, not borrowed.

Each one takes something we already own (the arrow in the logo, a receipt, the rails money moves on) and turns it into a quiet, moving texture that sits with the haze. Pick one, or mix. Nothing here is on the homepage until you approve it.

Option A

Arrow lines

Hairline stripes at 45°, the exact angle of the Upperlink arrow. A band of haze colour travels up the lines like money moving through, then fades.

Pattern · full strength

Behind a card

At half strength it reads as texture, never as decoration fighting the copy.

On the developer panel

On night it glows faintly, like a circuit.

Option B

Receipt stripes

Vertical stripes of varying weight, read from a receipt and an NQR code. They rise and settle in a slow wave, as if payments were landing one by one.

Pattern · full strength

Under a stat or quote

The wave can pulse when a number updates.

On night

Brighter stripes pick up the blue and red ends of the haze.

Option C

Arrowhead stripes

Rows of the arrowhead from the logo, set as a stripe and drifting up and to the right. The most branded of the three: unmistakably Upperlink.

Pattern · full strength

Behind a card

Works as a watermark for licences and receipts.

On night

Gives the dark panels a brand texture.
